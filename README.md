# Plants for Bees  
### Data-Driven Recommendations for Pollinator-Friendly Habitats

## Overview
This project analyzes ecological survey data to identify plant species that best support pollinator bees, with a focus on differences between native and non-native bee visitation.  
Through exploratory data analysis and lightweight modeling, the goal is to generate practical, evidence-based planting recommendations for conservation and habitat planning.

---

## Problem Statement
Pollinator bees are essential to ecosystem health, yet their populations are under pressure from habitat loss and environmental change.

This project aims to:
- Analyze plant–bee interaction patterns
- Compare plant support for native vs. non-native bees
- Identify plant species that promote both **bee abundance and diversity**

---

## Data Description
The dataset contains field observations across multiple land samples, including:
- Plant species
- Bee species and visitation counts
- Native vs. non-native bee classification
- Temporal and categorical context

Data preparation included cleaning, aggregation, and feature exploration. The dataset contains no missing values.

---

## Exploratory Data Analysis
EDA focused on:
- Bee abundance and species diversity across plant species
- Native vs. non-native bee visitation patterns
- Relationships between observation frequency, abundance, and diversity

Visualizations were used extensively to surface trends and guide interpretation.

---

## Methodology
- Aggregated and ranked plant species by bee visitation and diversity
- Compared plant performance across native and non-native bee groups
- Applied a Random Forest model to explore feature importance, highlighting the impact of **class imbalance** in ecological data

---

## Key Insights
- Native bees visit a broader range of plant species
- Plants supporting high species diversity are more valuable than those with high visitation alone
- Class imbalance can inflate model performance and must be interpreted carefully

---

## Recommendations
The analysis identifies a shortlist of plant species that:
- Attract high numbers of native bees
- Support diverse pollinator populations
- Are strong candidates for pollinator-friendly habitat design
